Additional Information
Additional forecast parameters through 7 days are available from the National Digital Forecast Database at http://weather.gov/forecasts/graphical/sectors/hawaii.php.

The National Digital Forecast Database is made available to all customers and partners from the public, private and academic sectors. Those customers and partners may use this data to create a wide range of text, graphic, gridded and image products of their own. For step by step instructions on how to get this data, visit https://www.weather.gov/mdl/ndfd_home.

Graphics may be slow to download depending on your internet connection.

We would appreciate any comments about these new products. Please email W-HFO.Webmaster@noaa.gov.
